MPCut includes an FFmpeg executable and libraries from the FFmpeg, SVT-AV1 and LAME projects.

The bundled FFmpeg 9.0.2 executable and libraries are distributed under the GNU Lesser
General Public License, version 2.1 or later. See COPYING.LGPLv2.1 and
LICENSE.md in this directory. The project site is https://ffmpeg.org/.

The FFmpeg codec library incorporates the SVT-AV1 4.3.0 encoder from the
Alliance for Open Media. Its upstream license, patent terms and additional
notices are in SVT-AV1/. The project site is https://gitlab.com/AOMediaCodec/SVT-AV1.

The FFmpeg codec library also incorporates the LAME 3.100 MP3 encoder, built
without its decoder or command-line frontend. LAME is distributed under the
GNU Library General Public License, version 2 or later. See LAME/COPYING and
LAME/LICENSE. The project site is https://lame.sourceforge.io/ and the source
archive is https://downloads.sourceforge.net/project/lame/lame/3.100/lame-3.100.tar.gz.

This directory includes the three upstream source archives, the patch to require
hardware decoding in VideoToolbox, and the build recipe. FFmpeg's shared
libraries are installed in Contents/Frameworks. The three video encoders
enabled for Smart Trim are h264_videotoolbox, hevc_videotoolbox and libsvtav1.
Re-encode uses the FFmpeg executable in Contents/Helpers/ffmpeg with the
h264_videotoolbox, hevc_videotoolbox and prores_videotoolbox video encoders
and native AAC and PCM audio encoders. Audio conversion also uses the
libmp3lame MP3 encoder and the native FLAC encoder.

To rebuild the executable and libraries on Apple Silicon with Xcode, CMake and pkg-config, create a build
directory with scripts/, packaging/, and .build/mpcut-ffmpeg/ subdirectories.
Copy build-ffmpeg.sh into scripts/, changes.diff into packaging/ as
ffmpeg-require-hardware.patch, and the source archives (the LAME archive is in
LAME/ and the SVT-AV1 archive in SVT-AV1/) into .build/mpcut-ffmpeg/.
Run `bash scripts/build-ffmpeg.sh` from the build directory. The resulting
libraries are in .build/mpcut-ffmpeg/install/lib/ and the executable is in
.build/mpcut-ffmpeg/install/bin/ffmpeg.
